About the Artist

م | the artist

◆

artquest.m is a self-taught artist whose work lives at the intersection of emotion and form. Every piece is an exploration — a conversation between the canvas and the soul.

Drawing from personal experience, nature, and the quiet moments between breaths, the work seeks to make the invisible visible — to give shape to what language cannot hold.

"I paint to remember. I paint to forget. The rest is just colour."
